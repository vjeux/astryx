- Widths are set as the PR's own spec sets them. After 300 ms, the list labels are read on 12 consecutive animation frames (the DOM).
- Shown text: for each still, the text the still shows is read right before and right after it. Each glyph counts as shown when at least half of it lies inside the still and every clipping box. A glyph also needs to sit before the ellipsis of an overflowing `text-overflow: ellipsis` box. A clipping box under a CSS transform (ScaledRow) is scaled to the page first.
- The first character of such a box is never ellipsed. When it does not fit with the ellipsis, Chromium draws it and a clipped ellipsis, and Firefox draws no ellipsis and clips the text.
- A box counts as overflowing when its text's natural width passes the box's computed content width, in layout px. The natural width is read from an untransformed copy in the same font and letter-spacing. The cut-off is 1.5/64 px in Chromium and 0.01 px in Firefox.
- In shown text, "cut from" means an ellipsis replaced the rest; "clipped from" means a box edge cut it off; "not shown" means no glyph is visible.
- The count-capped row has a second still, "-outlined": the capture draws a dashed 1 px red line just outside the list's box, as an overlay outside the story, so the list's width shows. The gap is measured in the page: the list's right edge minus its last item's right edge.
- ScaledRow at exact width, as the PR's own test, under the story's scale(0.5) and again under scale(0.9995) set on the frame: a letter-spacing goes on every scaled-wide button, in 0.005 px steps from 0 to 0.2 px. At each step the row's width is cleared, then set to the list's computed width. After 150 ms (when the PR's test reads) the list's items are read; then a still is taken once the screen is steady (two equal screenshots 100 ms apart), and the items are read again. A fold that comes after 150 ms shows in the still and in that second read only. The public set has, per engine, the stills at 0 px and, for each build, at its first step whose still shows "…, Archive, +1", lacks one of the six or cuts a label (all 41 steps are in the private bundle).
- ScaledRow as the story renders it: the same letter-spacing sweep with no width set on the row, which has room under the story's scale(0.5).
- Scale-in from 0.9, as the PR's own test: the frame's transform becomes scale(0.9) and the row takes the list's computed width. After 300 ms, three stills 100 ms apart; then the transform is removed and, after 300 ms, one more still. The same scale-in also runs on 5 fresh page loads, one still after each.
- Scale-in at 0.9995, as the PR's own test: with no transform, the list's computed width is read (unscaled). Then scale(0.9995) and a 699 px row; after 300 ms the list's computed width (the width it publishes) and items are read, and a still is taken; then the transform is removed.
- WideRow, as the PR's own test: a letter-spacing goes on every button in 12 steps of 1/40 px. At each step, after 250 ms, attribute changes on the list are counted for 500 ms, and its items and width are read. Stills at 0 and 0.275 px.
