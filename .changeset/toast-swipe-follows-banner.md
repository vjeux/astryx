[feat] Toast takes `presentation`, on `ToastViewport` (every toast it shows) and on `Toast`: `'adaptive'` (the default), `'toast'` or `'notification'`. Under `'adaptive'`, a toast under a finger (a coarse primary pointer, on every touch device, iOS and Android alike) now follows iOS notification banners, as `'notification'` does on every device. The card stays still for the first 14 px of finger travel and then follows at 0.55 of the finger, with no jump. On release, a quick flick toward the edge dismisses even when it barely moved the card, as does a drag that leaves the card far enough out; a release moving back toward rest, or anything else, springs the card back, and a drag that turns back away from the edge stretches the card the same way and springs it back. However a toast is dismissed (a swipe, the close button, the timeout, the app's dismiss), the card leaves as the banner does: it slides past its edge from where it is, continuing a flick's speed, without fading, and its row closes after it. While the card springs back or leaves, touches pass through to the page. Previously a toast under a finger followed Toast's own swipe, the card moving with the finger as it faded and shrank, and faded out when dismissed: `presentation="toast"` keeps that on every device. A mouse now swipes a toast too, as a finger and a pen do. Toast's own swipe (with a fine pointer, or `presentation="toast"`) runs on the shared gesture system: it begins after 10 px of travel toward the edge instead of 8; a flick dismisses at any travel, on the speed it lifts at, so a one-line toast can be flicked away and a pen that rests before it lifts does not flick; and a second finger no longer cancels a swipe that has begun, while one that lands before it begins leaves the touch to the page, so it can pinch. Under reduced motion nothing slides, springs or drifts: on a touch screen, or after a finger's release, the toast fades in place before its row closes, and a mouse or a pen with a fine pointer changes it at once.
