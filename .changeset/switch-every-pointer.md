[feat] Switch's press and drag work with a mouse and a pen as well as a finger: dragging past the flip point flips the switch, and the release commits wherever it happens. A mouse or pen press slid off the switch still leaves it alone, as a checkbox does. With a mouse the switch keeps its desktop look: the thumb slides to the side a drag flips it to, back at the press point it slides back, and the release commits the side it shows. On a touchscreen laptop a finger flips it by the same rule, with the desktop look.
