[feat] Carousel `hasPaging` shows one full-width child at a time. A swipe, flick, arrow key or button moves exactly one page. On a touch screen the pages follow the finger, rubber-band at the ends and settle on UIKit's paging curve, even while JavaScript is busy; with `hasLoop`, the page after the last is the first. With a mouse or trackpad the carousel snaps one page at a time.
