- Hosted previews only, nothing built locally. Each take reads `version.json` from its preview before and after the take:
  - main (production preview), https://astryx.atmeta.com: `{"commit":"9faffea27a24b639cc19ba1e77321e9e45931bc4","environment":"production"}`
  - a7bde721 (the PR's previous head, preview), https://astryx-git-feat-reorder-drop-glide-fbopensource.vercel.app: `{"commit":"a7bde7212424174f216413259ad101af21e6bbdd","environment":"preview"}`
  - 3329e0bf (the PR's head, preview), https://astryx-git-feat-reorder-drop-glide-fbopensource.vercel.app: `{"commit":"3329e0bf93f290dd9bf19f0bce4785209cf97ad9","environment":"preview"}`
- Story `lab-listinput--tag-options`, opened as `/storybook/iframe.html?id=lab-listinput--tag-options&viewMode=story`. Requests to other origins are blocked (none were made).
- Browser: Playwright chromium 149.0.7827.55, viewport 1400×900, deviceScaleFactor 2, light. Mouse input is real input through the DevTools protocol (Playwright's mouse: Input.dispatchMouseEvent).
- The drag: the pointer goes to the centre of the record's reorder handle and waits 300 ms. The button goes down; after 100 ms the pointer moves down in 20 steps, 16 ms apart, and rests for 700 ms. Then the button comes up (the release).
- First-record case: the first record, which shows the column labels, moves 150 px down. Second-record case: the second record moves 90 px down.
- Reorder handle boxes: main 28×28 px, a7bde721 16×16 px, 3329e0bf 16×16 px. The PR's base branch is feat/reorder-handle-options (#11575, not merged), which main does not have.
- Frames: a DevTools screencast (Page.startScreencast, PNG, every frame), page only, cropped to the list plus 8 px. The page renders at deviceScaleFactor 2; the screencast delivers each frame at 1 px per CSS px.
- A frame's time is its compositor swap time minus the time of the release's pointerup event, in ms. The public frames run from the last frame before the release to two frames after the settle. Each case ran 3 times in a fresh page; the frames are the first run's.
- Numbers: a requestAnimationFrame callback reads every record's box, its fields' boxes (the Color combobox and the Label input) and the column labels' boxes. Records are keyed by data-list-input-row. While the pointer is down, a preview copy of the record follows the pointer.
- All y and x values are CSS px from the top left of the page.

## Definitions

- Field jump at the release: how far a record's first field top moves from the last frame before the release to the first after. Positive is down. For the dragged record, "before" is the preview copy's field. A moved record that glides from where its fields were shown has a jump near 0.
- Largest one-frame field move: the largest change of a moving record's first field top between two frames in a row. It counts from the last frame before the release.
- Horizontal field misalignment: in each frame, each record's field left edge against its column label's left edge, as that frame shows the labels. The same offset on the first record at rest is subtracted. The value is the largest over all records and both fields.
- Settle: the time from the release to the first sample from which every sample is settled. In a settled sample, no preview copy is left and every record is at opacity 1. Every record's box and first field also sit within 0.5 px of their final y.
- Overshoot: a moved record's largest distance past its final field top, away from where its field was shown.
- Drawn under: a record's first field is under another record when the hit test at the field's centre (document.elementFromPoint) lands in that other record. The preview copy is inert, so the hit test passes through it and it is not tested.
- The a7bde721 takes have no hit test, because that preview is gone. Their "under" comes from boxes and list order: the field's centre lies inside the box of a record later in the list. In the head's and main's takes, that rule gives the hit test's answer in 4411 of 4412 record-frames.
- Message opacity: the opacity of the record's message text, times the opacity of each of its ancestors up to the list. The a7bde721 takes did not record it.
