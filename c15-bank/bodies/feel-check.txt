Checklist:
1. Tap each tab: the lens lifts into glass, slides to the tab and settles; the bar flexes a little under the finger.
2. Press and drag across the tabs: the lens follows the finger, the tab under it magnified; lift on a tab: it settles there.
3. Drag past the last tab, staying on the bar: the lens rubber-bands a little past it; lift: the last tab is chosen.
4. Drag off the bar (up, or past its end) and lift there: nothing is chosen and the lens goes back to the selected tab.
5. With a mouse or trackpad: drag the lens across and let go mid-drag: it follows the pointer and settles with the drag's momentum.

Checklist:
1. Minimize On Scroll: scroll the page down with a finger: the bar collapses into a circle; scroll back to the top, or tap the circle: it expands.
2. Bottom Accessory: scroll down: the accessory slides inline beside the circle; scroll up: it returns above the bar.
3. Search Tab: tap the search tab: the bar collapses and the field opens with the keyboard; tap Cancel: the circle shrinks back with a wobble and the bar returns.
4. More Tab: tap More: the list opens; choose a section: it opens inside More, which stays selected.
5. Disabled Tab: tap the disabled tab: nothing moves; drag over it and lift: nothing is chosen and the lens returns to the selected tab.
