`burst-settle --threshold T [--settle-frames N] [--key NAME] [--delta] [--format ffprobe|metadata|series] [--fps F] [--list] [--json] <file or ->` reads a screen recording's per-frame series and prints its motion bursts and the frame where each settles. Inputs: ffprobe's JSON (`ffprobe -f lavfi -i "movie=REC,signalstats" -show_entries frame=pts_time:frame_tags=KEY -of json`), ffmpeg's `metadata=print` text (`-vf signalstats,metadata=print:key=KEY:file=OUT`), or a series of `value` or `time value` lines (`#` starts a comment, so a header needs one; `--fps` gives a timeless series its times). The format is detected unless `--format` names it. `--key` picks the tag (default `lavfi.signalstats.YDIF`, the mean change from the previous frame). Without `--delta` each frame's value is its motion, as YDIF or a change energy is; with `--delta` the motion is the value's change from the previous frame, for a level such as `lavfi.signalstats.YAVG`. A frame MOVES when its motion is over the threshold. A burst starts at a moving frame and lasts until `--settle-frames` quiet frames (default 3) follow its last moving frame; it SETTLES at the first of them, and its PEAK is its first frame with the highest motion. Each burst prints as `burst K: from START to END, peak P at T; settles at S` (or `STILL MOVING`), then a summary; with no burst it names the highest motion. `--list` prints every frame, `--json` one JSON object. Every number is read with Rust's float parser, so `6.17284e-06` is six millionths; a value that does not parse, a frame without the key, mixed time columns or a time that goes back is an error with its place, never a skipped frame. Exit 0 when every burst settles (or there is none), 1 when a burst is still moving at the end, 2 on a usage, read or parse error.

Checked on recordings ffmpeg made (`tests/fixtures`): a box that moves twice gives bursts at frames 30–45 and 76–84, settling at 46 and 85, the same from the JSON and from the `metadata=print` text. One pixel that changes by one luma step in a 450×360 frame gives YDIF `6.17284e-06`: no burst at 0.5, and one at frame 30 at `1e-6`; the same value with its exponent dropped (`6.17284`, what a `[0-9.]+` pattern reads) makes a burst at 0.5. A fade read with `--key lavfi.signalstats.YAVG --delta` gives one burst at frames 31–39. std only; `cargo test --release` runs these and the parser cases.
